( 01 · Our story )

FutFront stands for Future Frontier. We help people build products that build their future.

Why we started

Too many founders and businesses pay a consultancy, an MSP or a dev shop for a product and get a half-finished handover. The code ships, the vendor moves on, and the real problems arrive after launch: features nobody validated, systems that won’t scale, bugs nobody owns. The client ends up solving them alone.

We started FutFront to be the partner we wish they’d had. One team that validates the idea, maps the product, builds it properly and stays accountable after it goes live, from the first whiteboard to the thousandth user.

Financial Markets01

An independent surveillance layer that flags front-running, manipulation and poor execution, and explains every alert.

  • Node.js
  • Next.js
  • ClickHouse
  • PostgreSQL
  • OpenAI
  • AWS

The problem

Every day, compliance officers at asset managers and portfolio-management houses have to decide which dealer and fund-manager orders look suspicious, and prove why. We automated the whole loop: ingest, detect, explain, follow up and file, with a human signing off on every case.

What we built

  • Ingests OMS order logs, exchange fill files (including password-protected PDFs) and broker contract notes from email, with formats auto-detected
  • 35+ detection rules over tick and bar data covering pre-trade leakage, market impact, reversion, round-trips, volatility spikes and shadow P&L, statistically calibrated to cut false alerts
  • Custom AND/OR alert builder with versioned, append-only thresholds, one-click rollback and backfill
  • AI explains each alert in plain English, checks whether public news explains a price move, drafts non-accusatory emails to dealers and reads their replies
  • Regulator-ready audit pack per order: trades, alerts, index comparison, corporate actions, news checks and the full email thread
  • Single-tenant deployment in the client’s own cloud with a tamper-proof audit trail, nightly restore checks and no vendor access to production

Impact

Financial Markets04

Every dealer call transcribed, decoded and risk-scored in English, Hindi, Gujarati and Hinglish.

  • Whisper
  • OpenAI
  • TypeScript
  • Next.js
  • Python
  • Redis

The problem

Recorded dealer calls have to be monitored for insider trading and front-running. But the calls are multilingual, code-switched and full of coded language that keyword tools miss.

What we built

  • Nine parallel AI analyses: synopsis, intent tags, code-word and secrecy markers, out-of-context segments, translation and a risk narrative
  • Composite 0–100 risk score with a recommended action, from no action to filing a suspicious transaction report
  • Org-specific codebook of suspicious phrases in three languages, with hit counts
  • Auto-alerts for high-risk calls, channel switching and insider intent, plus a per-employee AI deep-dive

Enterprise Platforms13

One front door for many AI agents, with routing, policy, verification and kill switches built in.

  • Python
  • Pydantic
  • AWS Bedrock
  • pgvector
  • OpenTelemetry
  • MCP

The problem

When a company ships several AI products owned by different teams, it needs one assistant with shared routing, entitlements, audit and cost control, without coupling every team to one codebase.

What we built

  • Router cascade (sticky → rules → calibrated classifier → small model → clarify), with a supervisor only for cross-product questions
  • Manifest contract enforced in CI: agents that read untrusted input get no write tools, writes are idempotent and depth is capped
  • Numbers verifier: no figure reaches the user until it’s checked against tool output
  • Taint-aware policy and a central switchboard where kill always wins
  • Append-only, forkable trajectory store with crypto-shredding
  • Statistical bench gates (Wilson, McNemar, calibration) as release criteria

( 10 · FAQ )

Good questions

What does FutFront stand for?

FutFront stands for Future Frontier. We help people build products that build their future, and we stay accountable after launch instead of handing over and moving on.

What does FutFront do?

FutFront is a zero-to-one product studio. We take ideas from market-fit validation and product mapping through design, AI and full-stack engineering to launch and scale, as one end-to-end partner.

Can you help before we have a product?

Yes, that is where we start. We validate the market, map the product and scope an MVP before writing code, so you invest in what customers will actually use.

What does an engagement look like?

Four phases: discover and validate, map the product, build and ship, then launch and scale. You can engage us for the full journey or for a single phase, such as a market-fit study or an MVP build.

Which industries does FutFront work with?

Capital markets and wealth management, enterprise data and SaaS analytics, telecom, government and deep tech. Our catalog includes trade surveillance, order management, data stewardship copilots and governed analytics assistants.

How do you make AI reliable?

We keep models away from the facts. Deterministic code computes every number, outputs are validated against schemas and sources, every write needs human confirmation, and every action lands in an audit trail.

Who founded FutFront?

FutFront was founded by IIT Bombay alumni Adesh Bhavsar, Ravi Kumar and Abhishek Malyan, with experience across Elevance Health, Trinity Life Sciences, Flexiloans, Omind Tech, HPCL and Mitacs research.

How do we start a project with FutFront?

Send us a note through the contact form, even if the idea is still rough. We set up a discovery call, look at the market and the problem, and propose the right first step.
